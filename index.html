    How it works

    Every day at 08:30 KST a GitHub Action re-reads the curated lists below, records the star count of every tracked repo, and ranks them by growth over the last 7 days. When less than a week of history exists, the oldest available snapshot is used — the window is shown next to each number. Data is plain JSON in the repo.
